# Logo Design Brief — Bump Match

## What is Bump Match?
A mobile app where expecting parents swipe on baby names (like Tinder) and get notified when both partners like the same name — a "match."

## Brand Feel
Warm, playful, modern, and premium. Think elevated millennial parent — not cutesy or clinical. Similar vibe to Peanut, Hatch, or Headspace.

## Current Visual Direction
- **Colors**: Soft pinks, mauves, corals (#C9817A, #D98CB8), with teal and lavender accents
- **Style**: Glassmorphism, soft gradients, rounded forms
- **Typography**: Serif wordmark — "Bump" bold, "Match" bold italic
- **Existing icon**: Simple heart shape in coral

## What We Need
1. **App icon** (1024x1024, works at all sizes, opaque background)
2. **Wordmark** ("Bump Match" lockup, works on light and dark backgrounds)
3. **Standalone icon mark** (no text, works as favicon/notification icon)
4. Deliver in SVG, PNG (2x/3x), and PDF

## Design Guidance
- Explore combining: heart, pregnant belly curve, two shapes meeting/overlapping
- Keep it minimal and rounded — avoid literal baby imagery (no rattles, storks, pacifiers)
- Gradients are on-brand; avoid neon, harsh red, or heavy black
- Must feel premium, not like a dating app
