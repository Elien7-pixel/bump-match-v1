## Full Description

Finding the perfect baby name just got easier — and way more fun.

Bump Match helps expecting parents discover baby names together using a simple swipe. Like a name? Swipe right. Not feeling it? Swipe left. When you and your partner both love the same name, it's a match.

HOW IT WORKS
- Browse beautiful name cards showing meaning, origin, and cultural background
- Swipe right to like, left to pass — undo anytime
- Connect with your partner using a QR code or invite code
- See your matches instantly when you both like the same name

FIND NAMES TOGETHER
Connect with your partner and swipe independently. Bump Match tracks your preferences in real time and highlights the names you agree on — no more endless debates.

14 LANGUAGES & CULTURES
Discover names from Afrikaans, English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Greek, Latin, and more. Every name comes with its meaning and cultural origin.

FILTER YOUR WAY
Browse boy names, girl names, or gender-neutral names. Switch languages anytime to explore names from different cultures.

TRACK YOUR FAVORITES
All your liked names are saved in one place. Matches with your partner are highlighted so you can easily see which names made the cut.

YOUR NAMES, YOUR CHOICE
- Private and secure — only you and your partner see your picks
- Works on phones and tablets
- Dark mode supported
