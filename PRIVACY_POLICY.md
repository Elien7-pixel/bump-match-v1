- We collect information you give us and information about how you use the App so

## 5. Selling and sharing data with partners (opt-in only)

**What this means.** If — and only if — you switch on **Partner Offers**, we may
disclose, share, or **sell** your **name and contact details** (email and/or phone)
to third-party partners, including **companies that market and sell baby, pregnancy,
and parenting products**. Those partners may contact you with offers and may use the
data for their own marketing.

Please note: because Bump Match is a pregnancy/parenting app, receiving your details
from us may allow a partner to **infer that you are an expecting or new parent.** We
want you to understand this before you opt in.

**This is entirely optional:**
- It is **OFF by default.**
- You can use **every feature** of the App without turning it on.
- You can turn it **on or off at any time** in **Settings → Privacy → Partner Offers.**
- Turning it off stops future sharing. To ask partners to delete data already
  shared, contact us and we will pass on your request.
