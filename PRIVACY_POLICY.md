## 1. Summary (the important part)

- We collect information you give us and information about how you use the App so

- **We can sell or share some of your personal information (your name and contact
  details) with third-party partners — including companies that sell baby and

## 5. Selling and sharing data with partners (opt-in only)

**What this means.** If — and only if — you switch on **Partner Offers**, we may
disclose, share, or **sell** your **name and contact details** (email and/or phone)
to third-party partners, including **companies that market and sell baby, pregnancy,
and parenting products**. Those partners may contact you with offers and may use the
data for their own marketing.

Please note: because Bump Match is a pregnancy/parenting app, receiving your details
from us may allow a partner to **infer that you are an expecting or new parent.** We
want you to understand this before you opt in.

**This is entirely optional:**
- It is **OFF by default.**
- You can use **every feature** of the App without turning it on.
- You can turn it **on or off at any time** in **Settings → Privacy → Partner Offers.**
- Turning it off stops future sharing. To ask partners to delete data already
  shared, contact us and we will pass on your request.

We provide the list of current partner categories (and, on request, named partners)
on request via **ai@sherbetagency.com**.

## 7. Advertising and measurement

We use two third-party measurement tools inside the App and on our website.

**a. Analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase**

We use Google Analytics (via Firebase) to understand how the App is used: which
screens people visit, which features they use, whether they complete sign-up, and
how many people return. This tells us what to fix and what to build next.

For this we send Google an app-instance identifier, your account identifier, device
and technical data, and coarse attributes such as an **age band** (not your exact
age), your general country, and whether you have connected a partner.

We do **not** send Google your pregnancy stage, due date, expected baby gender,
cultural heritage, the names you swipe on, or your matches.

**b. Advertising — Meta (Facebook and Instagram)**

We advertise Bump Match on Meta's platforms. To measure whether those adverts work,
and to reach people who resemble our existing users, the App tells Meta when certain
milestones happen — that you installed the App, created an account, started swiping,
connected a partner, or reached a match.

To help Meta recognise you as an existing user, we also send a **cryptographically
hashed** (scrambled, one-way) version of your email address and name. This is called
advanced matching. Hashing is a safeguard, not anonymisation — please treat this as
sharing personal information with Meta.

We do **not** send Meta your pregnancy stage, due date, expected baby gender,
cultural heritage, the names you swipe on, or your matches. This is enforced in the
App's code, not only by policy.

**What Meta does with it.** Meta uses these signals to report on our adverts and to
build **lookalike audiences** — groups of people who resemble our users, so we can
advertise to them. Meta acts as an independent controller for its own advertising
purposes; see Meta's own privacy policy for how it handles data it receives.

**c. Advertising identifiers**

On iOS we ask for permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier,
via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. **If you decline, we do not use it, and
the App continues to work exactly the same.** On Android the equivalent identifier is
controlled by your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

**d. Our website**

bumpmatch.app uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure visits and how
many people tap through to the app stores.

**e. Legal bases**
- **Consent** — for advertising identifiers, advanced matching, and building
  advertising audiences. On iOS your ATT choice is that consent.
- **Legitimate interests** — for basic product analytics that do not use advertising
  identifiers, so that we can keep the App working and improve it.

**f. How to opt out**
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or the Bump Match entry in
  iOS Settings.
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
- **Any platform:** email ai@sherbetagency.com and we will exclude your account
  from advertising audiences.

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

- **Access** a copy of your data.

- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Port** your data to another service.

honor **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** browser/device signals where required.

To exercise any right, contact **ai@sherbetagency.com**. We will respond within
the timeframe required by law (generally 30–45 days). You also have the right to
complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your local
authority).
